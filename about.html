Our Story

A Business Built Around the Need to Keep Things Moving

Every business depends on movement. Raw materials need to reach factories. Finished products need to reach customers. Machinery needs to move between locations. Containers, commercial goods and industrial cargo need to reach their destinations at the right time.

Balaji Trans Logistics was established to serve that fundamental need — reliable transportation for businesses.

Led by Proprietor Pramila Sharma, Balaji Trans Logistics is building its presence from Bengaluru with a clear focus on transportation and logistics for commercial and industrial requirements.

The business focuses on key industrial locations including Peenya, Bommasandra, Jigani, Dabaspet and Devanahalli, while supporting transportation requirements to destinations based on the customer's cargo and route requirements.

Rather than trying to be everything to everyone, BTL is focused on doing what businesses need most from a transportation partner: understanding the requirement, arranging a suitable transportation solution and helping move the cargo towards its destination.

BTL is a proprietor-led logistics company in Bengaluru, focused on commercial and industrial transportation rather than generic one-size-fits-all claims.

The work is practical: goods transport, cargo transportation, trailer and container movement, and support for industrial logistics around Bangalore.

A growing transportation company with a clear focus on business cargo.

Every shipment is different. Cargo type, load size, pickup location, destination and timing all shape the transportation solution.

BTL starts by understanding the requirement, then identifying a suitable option — full truck load, part load, trailer, container or specialised movement.

Understand the requirement, then arrange the right transport.

Transportation can also be arranged to other destinations based on the cargo and route requirement.
