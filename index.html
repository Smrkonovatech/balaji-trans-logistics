About Balaji Trans Logistics

Reliable Transportation. Practical Logistics.

Balaji Trans Logistics is a transportation and logistics company serving businesses across Bengaluru and key industrial locations. We provide transportation solutions for goods, cargo, vehicles, containers, trailers, heavy machinery and other commercial requirements.

Our focus is simple: provide dependable transportation solutions that help businesses move their goods efficiently from one location to another.

BTL supports manufacturers, suppliers, warehouses, commercial businesses and industrial customers with flexible transportation options.

Dependable movement of goods, cargo and industrial consignments.

We plan transportation around the cargo, pickup point, destination and timeline you share with us.

Options include full truck load, part load, trailer, container, car and specialised cargo movement.

Flexible transport options for industrial and commercial requirements.

BTL focuses on Peenya, Bommasandra, Jigani, Dabaspet and Devanahalli, along with surrounding Bengaluru industrial routes.
